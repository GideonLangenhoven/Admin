The Reviews page is where you moderate customer reviews before they appear publicly.

## Moderating

Filter by status: **Pending** (submitted, awaiting moderation), **Approved**, **Hidden**, and **Spam**. Each review shows its source, star rating, comment, reviewer, and tour. Use **Approve** to publish, **Hide** to remove from public view, or **Spam** to mark junk. Acting on a review moves it out of the current filter view.

## Where reviews come from

Reviews arrive from customer review requests (sent automatically after trips by the auto-message system) and other configured sources such as Google reviews, which sync daily.
