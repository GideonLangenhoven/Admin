The Operators page (at /operators) controls which business your dashboard is currently showing, for admins who manage more than one operator.

## Switching operator

If your account has access to multiple operators, each is listed with a **Switch** button. Switching re-scopes the entire dashboard — bookings, refunds, pricing, reports, media — to the chosen operator. If you only have one operator, it shows as locked and there's nothing to switch.

## Data isolation

Each operator's data is fully isolated. Switching context is the only way to see another operator's bookings, and only if your account has been granted access to that operator.
