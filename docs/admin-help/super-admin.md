The Super Admin page is platform-level control, visible only to SUPER_ADMIN accounts.

## Business management

List every operator business on the platform; suspend or reactivate subscriptions, adjust admin seat limits, edit subdomains, and open per-business detail. New tenants are onboarded from here — the onboarding workspace creates the business, its subdomain, and the first admin user, and sends the invite email.

## Other tools

Landing page management (with deploy), cross-tenant email usage and billing overview, and chatbot avatar management.
