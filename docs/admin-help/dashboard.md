The Dashboard is your at-a-glance cockpit for today: passengers, revenue, and anything needing attention.

## Today's numbers

The hero band shows **Today's Pax** (total passengers booked today — click it to open Bookings) and a **Revenue** card with today, last-7-days, and this-month totals plus a daily sparkline (click through to Reports for detail). All figures are in your business's timezone.

## Action cards

Three cards surface work needing attention, each linking to the right page:

- **Pending Refunds** — count and total value of refund requests waiting for a decision. Click to open the Refund Queue.
- **Inbox Action** — conversations where a customer is waiting for a human reply. Click to open the Inbox.
- **Photos Out** — recent trips that haven't had photos sent to guests yet. Click to open Trip Photos.

## Manifest and roll call

The **Manifest** panel lists passengers per slot for today or tomorrow (use the Today/Tomorrow toggle), so you can see how full each departure is. **Roll Call** shows the passenger list. Use the "Add Booking" shortcut to create a booking for a walk-in.

## Weather

The Weather panel shows conditions for your location — useful when deciding whether to run, and pairs with the weather-cancel tools under Slots and Broadcasts.
