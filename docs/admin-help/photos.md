Trip Photos sends photos from a trip to the guests who were on it, along with a thank-you message. Reach it from the Dashboard's "Photos Out" card or directly at /photos.

## Sending photos

1. Select a recent trip (bookings from the last 7 days, grouped by date).
2. Supply the photos either by **uploading to Google Drive** (only offered when Google Drive is connected under Settings → Integration Credentials — a per-trip folder is created automatically) or by **pasting photo URLs** (individually or in bulk).
3. **Send Photos to Lead Bookers** — each lead booker gets an email with the photos, and a WhatsApp message asking them to reply YES to receive the link (this respects WhatsApp's 24-hour messaging rule).

A progress bar shows sending status, and the trip is logged so the Dashboard stops counting it as "photos outstanding".

## Recently sent

The gallery at the bottom shows recently sent trips with their photo folders.
