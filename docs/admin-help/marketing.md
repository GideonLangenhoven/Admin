Marketing is your email-marketing hub: contacts, templates, campaigns, automations, and promotions, with an analytics overview.

## The overview page

The landing page shows Active Contacts, Templates, Emails Sent, and Campaigns, an **Email Usage This Month** meter (emails sent versus your plan's included quota, with overage cost projection), and charts for engagement (open, click, unsubscribe, bounce rates), campaign performance, audience breakdown, and open-rate trend.

## Contacts

Manage your audience under Marketing → Contacts. Contacts can be tagged (automation tag rules in Settings can tag them automatically based on booking behaviour). Unsubscribed contacts are excluded from all future sends automatically.

## Templates and campaigns

Design reusable email templates under Marketing → Templates, then send campaigns to a chosen audience. A scheduled campaign can be **cancelled** and a sending campaign **paused** from the Recent Campaigns table on the overview.

## Automations

Under Marketing → Automations you can set up triggered emails — for example a message a number of days before or after a booking date, birthday offers, or win-back campaigns. Automations support date-field triggers with day offsets and can generate one-off promo codes per recipient.

## Promotions

Marketing → Promotions manages promo codes that customers can apply at checkout and that you can apply on manual bookings.

## Sending quota

Email sending draws from your plan's monthly included quota; the usage meter warns before you go into overage. Details of your plan are on the Billing page.
