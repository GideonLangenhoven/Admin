Open **AI** in the Growth group, then the **AI Usage** tab. MAIN_ADMIN and SUPER_ADMIN can view customer chatbot usage and open the **Chat FAQ** tab from here.

## Replies, allowance and overage

The page shows this month's billable **Replies**, **Balance**, **Allowance used**, **Overage** and **Cache hits**, plus activity by surface. It refreshes every 30 seconds. The included reply allowance and per-reply overage rate come from your business's current configuration; read the displayed values instead of assuming every operator has the same plan or rate.

Customer WhatsApp and website AI replies count toward the allowance. Dashboard **Admin help chat**, intent/date parsing and shadow activity are labelled not billed. Quick Answers and the dashboard product guide are separate from each other.

## When the included allowance is used

The customer assistant can continue beyond its included replies at the displayed overage rate, until the configured hard ceiling shown on the page. At that ceiling, AI replies pause; existing bookings, reminders and payments are unaffected. Contact BookingTours to discuss an allowance change. Usage belongs in the platform subscription invoice, not a guest booking invoice.

## Updating what the guest bot knows

Use [Chat FAQ](/settings/chat-faq) for enabled keyword/intent rules and exact approved replies. Use [Operations & AI Configuration](/settings#operations) for FAQs, guest arrival/packing instructions and the chatbot's personality and business knowledge. Those settings feed the customer website and WhatsApp bot, not the dashboard help assistant.

## ChatGPT, MCP and Meta Muse

External operator AI connections are not customer-available. A local implementation or an AI agent connections screen is not evidence that a live connection is enabled. There is no verified working live Server URL to recommend, and Meta Muse compatibility is not verified. Ask platform support about availability; do not provide connection or credential setup steps as if this feature were live.
