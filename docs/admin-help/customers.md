The Customers page (at /customers — reached by link, it's not in the sidebar) is a read-only directory of everyone who has booked with you, ranked by lifetime trips.

## What it shows

Header cards give your **total customer count** and how many are **opted in** to marketing. The table lists name, email, phone, number of trips, total spent, last trip date, and marketing consent. Search by name, email, or phone.

## What it's for

It's informational — there are no edit actions here. To email customers, use Marketing (contacts are managed there); to message an individual, find their booking and use the WhatsApp action, or search for them in the Inbox.
