Open **Partners** in the Growth group to reach **Partners & Combos**. This area requires MAIN_ADMIN or SUPER_ADMIN.

## Partnerships

Enter another operator's admin email and choose **Invite Partner**. Only the invited operator can accept an incoming invitation. The email link opens a confirmation page; enter the email address that received it and choose **Accept partnership**. Opening the link alone does not accept it. If email delivery fails, the page says so and the invitation remains available on the partner's dashboard.

Either operator can **Revoke** the partnership, which deactivates its shared combo offers and stops new combo checkouts. Existing bookings and settlement records are kept. You can invite the same operator again after revocation; the new invitation needs fresh acceptance, and old email links no longer work. Offers remain inactive until deliberately activated again. A partnership does not give either operator unrestricted access to the other's customer records.

## Combo offers

Choose **New Combo Offer**, name the offer, set its per-person price, select the participating tours and enter their shares. You can bundle your own tours without another operator, or combine your tours with an active partner's tours. Review the included activities, shares and guest timing before activating an offer.

If the page says **Combo deals are not switched on yet**, you can prepare partnerships and offers, but customers will not see combo sales until the platform enables the feature. A saved offer alone does not confirm customer availability.

## Settlements

The settlement area lists combo amounts payable to or receivable from each partner. **Mark Settled** records a settlement already made; it does not transfer money. Add a reference or note so the record can be reconciled. Where offered, generating a settlement payment link can email the partner a checkout link; paid status updates when payment is confirmed. Review the result before assuming the partner received a link.

The [Reports](/reports) Financials tab also offers **Combo Settlements CSV** for accounting.
