Payment Reminders automates chasing unpaid bookings so you don't have to.

## Turning it on

The feature is off by default. Enable "send payment reminders & auto-cancel unpaid bookings" and choose the auto-cancel window — how many hours before the trip an unpaid booking is cancelled automatically (1 to 168 hours, default 12). Reminders with the payment link go out automatically while the booking is unpaid.

## The list

The page lists upcoming bookings that are unpaid but hold a payment link. Bookings inside the auto-cancel window show a warning ("cancels within N hours") so you can intervene before the system does.

## Letting a specific booking through

Sometimes you want a booking to stand without payment — a corporate client paying on invoice, or a regular you trust. Use **Allow without payment** on that booking; it's excluded from reminders and auto-cancel. **Require payment** puts it back under the normal rules.
