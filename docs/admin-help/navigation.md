The dashboard sidebar is organised into groups. Click a group label to collapse or expand it — the dashboard remembers your layout.

## Where to find each feature

- **Operations**: Bookings (the master booking list), New Booking (create a booking manually), Payment Reminders (automated unpaid-booking chasing), Slots (create and manage bookable time slots).
- **Customers**: Inbox (WhatsApp and web-chat conversations), Refunds (approve or decline refund requests), Vouchers (gift and credit vouchers), Reviews (moderate customer reviews).
- **Revenue**: Invoices (customer pro forma invoices), Peak Pricing (seasonal rates), Reports (exportable reporting), Billing (your subscription — admins only).
- **Growth**: Marketing (email campaigns, contacts, templates, automations), Broadcasts (message all guests on selected slots).
- **Admin**: Chat FAQ, Settings, Data Requests, Super Admin — these are visible to MAIN_ADMIN and above only.

Some pages are not in the sidebar and are reached from links: Customers directory (/customers), Trip Photos (/photos, from the Dashboard "Photos Out" card), Failed Notifications (/notifications), Operator switching (/operators), and the Guide check-in app (/guide).

## Roles and what you can see

There are four roles, lowest to highest: OPERATOR, ADMIN, MAIN_ADMIN, SUPER_ADMIN.

- OPERATOR and ADMIN handle day-to-day operations: bookings, inbox, slots, refunds, broadcasts, reports.
- MAIN_ADMIN additionally sees Billing, Settings, Chat FAQ, and Data Requests, and can manage admin users and integration credentials.
- SUPER_ADMIN is platform-level and can manage all operator businesses.
- An ADMIN can be granted access to specific Settings sections by a MAIN_ADMIN (per-section settings permissions) without getting full admin rights.

If you can't see a page mentioned in this help, your role doesn't include it — ask your main admin.

## The sidebar collapsed to icons

Click the collapse control at the bottom of the sidebar to switch between the full sidebar and a compact icon rail. Your choice is remembered on this device.

## Suspended subscription

If your business subscription is suspended, most pages are blocked and only Reports, Invoices, Refunds and Settings remain reachable, so you can still export data and settle outstanding refunds. A MAIN_ADMIN can manage the subscription under Billing.
