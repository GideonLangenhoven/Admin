The dashboard sidebar is organised into groups. Click a group label to collapse or expand it — the dashboard remembers your layout.

## Where to find each feature

- **Operations**: [Bookings](/bookings), [New Booking](/new-booking), and [Slots](/slots). Payment Reminder, Allow Without Payment and Require Payment are actions on a booking, not a separate Payment Reminders page.
- **Guest care**: [Inbox](/inbox) and [Refunds](/refunds).
- **Revenue**: [Invoices](/invoices), [Peak Pricing](/pricing), [Reports](/reports), and [Billing](/billing). Billing requires MAIN_ADMIN or SUPER_ADMIN. Reviews is a tab under Reports, at /reviews.
- **Growth**: [Marketing](/marketing), [Broadcasts](/broadcasts), [Partners](/partnerships), and [AI](/ai-usage). Partners and AI require MAIN_ADMIN or SUPER_ADMIN. Vouchers is a tab under Marketing, at /vouchers. Chat FAQ is a tab under AI, at /settings/chat-faq. Failed Notifications is a main-admin-only tab under Broadcasts, at /notifications.
- **Admin**: [Settings](/settings) and, for platform staff only, [Super Admin](/super-admin). Data Requests is reached from Settings, at /privacy/data-requests.

Use **Simple View** in the dashboard header to open the phone and tablet workflow at /simple. Use **Today** for booked sessions and arrivals, **Add walk-in** for a new booking, and **Full dashboard** to return to /.

Other direct links include [Customers](/customers), [Trip Photos](/photos) from the Dashboard's **Photo upload backlog** card, [Operators](/operators), and the [Guide app](/guide).

## Roles and what you can see

There are four roles, lowest to highest: OPERATOR, ADMIN, MAIN_ADMIN, SUPER_ADMIN.

- OPERATOR and ADMIN handle day-to-day operations: bookings, inbox, slots, refunds, broadcasts and the reporting or marketing areas their main admin has left available.
- MAIN_ADMIN additionally manages Billing, AI, Chat FAQ, Partners, Data Requests, Failed Notifications, admin users and integration credentials.
- SUPER_ADMIN is platform-level and can manage all operator businesses.
- An operations admin can be granted individual Settings sections: Tours & Activities, Booking Add-Ons, External Booking, Booking Site Config, Email Customisation and Invoice Details. Booking Site Config also grants Embed Widget and Operations & AI Configuration. Integration credentials and banking details remain main-admin-only.

Main admins can hide the dashboard revenue panel, Reports, Marketing and Reviews for individual staff members. A missing page can reflect these permissions as well as the account's role.

If you can't see a page mentioned in this help, your role doesn't include it — ask your main admin.

## The sidebar collapsed to icons

On desktop, use the collapse control beside the business name at the top of the sidebar to switch to an icon rail. On phones and tablets, use the bottom Dashboard, Bookings, New Booking and Inbox navigation, and **More** for the remaining pages.

The help assistant is opened from **AI help** in the dashboard header. In the shared demo the control is named **AI bot**. The assistant explains the product and can open pages; it does not read your bookings or account totals.

## Integrations that are not available yet

Direct Viator and GetYourGuide connections are not available yet. Settings shows this status; /settings/ota and /ota-drift are not available operational pages. ChatGPT/MCP and Meta Muse operator connections are not customer-available either. Do not treat locally developed connection screens as a working live integration.

## Suspended subscription

If the subscription is PAUSED or SUSPENDED, the current sign-in gate blocks normal dashboard pages. A MAIN_ADMIN can use **Go to Billing** to reach /billing; other staff should ask their main admin. SUPER_ADMIN support access is separate. For exports or outstanding refunds that cannot be reached while blocked, contact BookingTours support instead of assuming Reports, Invoices or Refunds remain accessible.
