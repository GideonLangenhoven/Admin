The Guide app (at /guide) is a mobile-friendly check-in tool for guides on the ground. It runs full-screen without the admin sidebar — designed for a phone at the meeting point.

## What guides can do

Pick a date to see that day's departures: time, tour, guest and booking counts, and how full each slot is. Tap a slot to see its passenger list and **check guests in** as they arrive. There's also a photos section for capturing trip photos.

## Access

Any dashboard account (OPERATOR role or above) can use the Guide app. Add it to the phone's home screen for app-like access — it works as a PWA.

## Check-ins and the office

Check-ins made in the Guide app show up on the main dashboard immediately, so the office sees who has arrived.
