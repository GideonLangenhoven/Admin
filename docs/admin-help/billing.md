Billing manages your BookingTours subscription — your plan, seats, and email overage charges. (Customer invoices live on the Invoices page; this is what you pay, not what customers pay.) Visible to MAIN_ADMIN and above.

## Your plan

The plan card shows your plan name, base price plus per-seat price, seats purchased versus admins actually active, this month's total, billing cycle, and status (Active, Paused, Cancelled, Suspended, or Trial).

## Seats

Each additional admin user seat costs **R500/month**.

**Add seat** or **Remove seat** adjusts how many admin users you're paying for, with a prorated charge or credit estimate shown before you confirm. You can't remove seats below the number of admins currently active — remove the admin user first under Settings → Admin Users.

## Pausing for the off-season

**Pause for off-season** stops normal billing and trading. The current sign-in gate shows **Your account is paused** and blocks normal dashboard pages; a MAIN_ADMIN can follow **Go to Billing** and use **Resume now**. Staff should ask their main admin to resume the subscription. Do not assume all dashboard pages remain readable during a pause.

## Plan changes and suspension

If other plans are offered, use **Change plan** and review the new included seats and proration before confirming. The current plan is shown once, with other distinct plans listed as alternatives. The plan card shows the actual configured rates. A suspended account also blocks normal dashboard access; its main admin can reach Billing, but operational suspension may need BookingTours support to resolve. Paying an invoice does not by itself guarantee that an operational suspension is lifted.

## Email usage

Marketing email usage shows any overage charges for the billing period and the rate for additional emails.

Customer chatbot reply usage is shown under [AI Usage](/ai-usage). AI and email overage belong to the platform subscription invoice. Dashboard help does not count toward the customer AI reply allowance.
