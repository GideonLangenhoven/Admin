Billing manages your BookingTours subscription — your plan, seats, and payment history. (Customer invoices live on the Invoices page; this is what you pay, not what customers pay.) Visible to MAIN_ADMIN and above.

## Your plan

The plan card shows your plan name, base price plus per-seat price, seats purchased versus admins actually active, this month's total, billing cycle, and status (Active, Paused, Cancelled, Suspended, or Trial).

## Seats

**Add seat** or **Remove seat** adjusts how many admin users you're paying for, with a prorated charge or credit estimate shown before you confirm. You can't remove seats below the number of admins currently active — remove the admin user first under Settings → Admin Users.

## Pausing for the off-season

**Pause for off-season** stops billing while keeping sign-in and read access; new bookings, marketing, and broadcasts are disabled while paused. **Resume now** brings everything back.

## Email usage

The marketing email meter shows emails sent against your plan's included quota and any overage charges.

## History and payment method

Your payment method on file and billing history are at the bottom of the page.
