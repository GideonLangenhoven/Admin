The Invoices page holds the pro forma invoices generated for customer bookings. This is customer paperwork — for your own BookingTours subscription billing, see the Billing page.

## Browsing and printing

Invoices are grouped by day; filter by date to find a specific one. Each invoice can be **printed or saved as PDF** (a print-ready document opens) or downloaded as HTML. Invoice numbers are assigned automatically and stay stable once generated.

## Company details on invoices

Company and EFT banking details come from [Invoice & Banking Details](/settings#invoice). A MAIN_ADMIN can manage both. Staff granted **Invoice Details** can edit the permitted company fields; banking details still require MAIN_ADMIN or SUPER_ADMIN.

## Resending an invoice

To resend an invoice to a customer, use the resend action on the booking itself on the Bookings page.
