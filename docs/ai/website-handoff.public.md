## Recommended product descriptions

| Area | Wording the website can use after editorial approval | Evidence limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Booking interface | “A branded booking interface organized around tours, dates and departures.” | Demo interface checks establish presentation, not sales results or production capacity at scale |
| Capacity | “Departure-based seat capacity, with existing bookings and holds reflected in availability.” | Availability is a changing snapshot; a read reserves no seats |
| WhatsApp and website chat | “Booking assistance and human handover, subject to each operator's configured channel and payment setup.” | Local tests do not establish every operator's live provider setup, response time or language accuracy |
| Yoco | “Yoco checkout support for Rand payments; each merchant's setup must be verified.” | Checkout support is not a settlement-speed, certification or completed-payment claim |
| Weather recovery and self-service | “Operator-led weather cancellation and configured customer recovery options.” | No autonomous weather decisions, recovery percentage or instant refund promise |
| Waivers | “Booking-specific digital waiver forms and booking-level status.” | This does not certify each participant's signature or legal enforceability |
| Marketing and CRM | “Configured email campaigns, contact workflows and selected-audience broadcasts.” | Sender setup, consent, opt-outs, quotas and provider rules apply |
| Pricing | “Operator-configured peak and seasonal rates, plus timed last-minute deals.” | These are configured rules; avoid AI demand-optimization claims |
| Photos | “Trip photo upload and sharing through a configured Google Drive connection.” | A team member uploads/shares; automatic capture or delivery was not established |

The calendar/catalogue have recorded live demo checks. Most other review evidence consists of local tests or code inspection. A demo is illustrative; it is not evidence of real revenue, conversion lift, universal integration availability or whole-platform production qualification.

## Attribution coordination

Track demo clicks, accepted leads, operator account creation, trial activation and confirmed operator subscription payments as separate milestones. Traveller tour payments are not operator subscriptions; recurring monthly invoices are not new acquired operators.

The application has a locally tested consent-aware campaign-tag helper and a read-only report for recorded billing/audit outcomes. It has no completed attribution flow joining website visits to operator acquisition. Account-created, demo-click and accepted-lead coverage are not available in that report. No real conversion counts were produced.

Reuse the website’s maintained product-facts contract and approved commercial wording; this application review does not supersede owner approvals or prove merchant-specific operation. Both repositories need a shared campaign allowlist, explicit analytics consent and a coordinated opaque intake reference stored through a server-side handoff. Separate deployments must not assume shared cookies or local storage. Keep contact details and credentials out of URLs. A ChatGPT/Muse campaign tag is evidence of a tag, not proof of a platform recommendation. Unknown traffic remains unknown; connecting an agent is separate from email-marketing consent.
