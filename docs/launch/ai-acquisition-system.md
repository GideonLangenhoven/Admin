# BookingTours: AI-led acquisition system

17 September 2026. Target: **1,000 distinct paying businesses**. Initial cash ceiling: **R3,000/month**. Founder preference: **20 minutes of recording per week, plus qualified sales calls; minimise everything else**. Social accounts and the business calendar have not yet been created.

This is the current operating plan. It supersedes the founder workload, initial acquisition target and cash allocations in [the original rollout](first-1000-users-lean-rollout.md). That document remains the source for product limitations, initial prospect research, referrals and conditional long-term growth arithmetic.

## The decision

Let AI own research, preparation, repurposing, record keeping and analysis. Use existing scheduling and business tools for delivery. Keep the founder close to the first customers so we learn what actually makes an owner pay and stay.

We are building a repeatable business process, not a custom autonomous-sales application. An AI-authored post is an asset; a scheduled post is an action; a paid, active business is an outcome. Track them separately.

### Founder time, honestly

For the first ten customers, allow approximately **6–8 hours/week**, not just the recording:

- 20 minutes recording answers to that week's four prompts.
- About two hours for four qualified calls with preparation/handover.
- Up to three hours for two early assisted onboardings.
- 30 minutes for a batch review of facts, offers and content.
- Remaining time for customer exceptions and early care.

The goal is to reduce this as setup becomes repeatable. Two to three weekly hours becomes plausible only if onboarding/support is demonstrably handled by the product, customer or a funded helper. Neither AI nor partner introductions eliminate payment-provider setup and customer support.

At four held qualified calls/week × an assumed 30% close × 80% activation, the initial capacity is about **four activations/month before free periods, collection failures and churn**. A sensible 90-day experiment target is **8–12 activated businesses, with 6–10 actually paying by day 90**, allowing for setup time and the site's founding-cohort free month. These are unvalidated targets, not forecasts. The prior 20–25 target is a stretch requiring more qualified calls, a better measured conversion rate or partner-assisted sales/setup. No part of the 1,000-business objective has been redefined as signups or followers.

My blunt application: **You need owners who will trust you with their booking operations. AI should make it cheaper to demonstrate value, answer questions and follow through. It cannot manufacture that trust by multiplying generic posts.**

Our adaptation is deliberately different from a large creator's publishing operation. Start with a small repeatable content batch and actual distribution to buyers; earn higher volume with results. Do not import revenue guarantees, inflated bonus values or invented scarcity into this offer.

## One offer and one conversion path

**Audience:** South African owner-led activity businesses with existing direct enquiries, simple scheduled departures and a recurring booking/admin problem. First cluster: Cape Town surf lessons, kayak trips and standard guided day hikes.

**Offer copy:** “Show us how one activity gets booked. We'll identify the manual steps, demonstrate the BookingTours workflow and, if it fits, help you set it up.”

Planning price is the R2,000 monthly base, but extra seats, seasonal pause, allowances, provider costs and tax treatment must be reconciled before a binding quote. The original rollout records the conflicts. Do not run price-led creative until that is done.

The existing [founding-operator programme](https://bookingtours.co.za/operators) already promises the first month free, assisted migration and a twelve-month base-price lock for a small cohort, in exchange for feedback and a later case study. Confirm remaining capacity and written eligibility before promoting it; the published five-place count is not verified inventory. Honour existing commitments. Do not stack the referral free month or other incentives without agreed terms. A free activated operator is not a paying business until a subscription payment is collected.

**Path:** owner sees a useful post/introduction → requests CHECK → receives the complete checklist → optionally requests a tailored review → answers qualification questions → qualified call → written quote → assisted activation → collected subscription → retention/reference/referral.

Requesting the checklist authorises delivery of that requested resource, not indefinite marketing. Give useful material before proposing a call. Ask separately whether they want a review and further relevant follow-up.

**Five qualification questions:**

1. What activity do you operate and where?
2. How many direct bookings/enquiries do you handle in a typical month?
3. Which step currently takes the most time or loses track of customers?
4. Do you need deposits, different passenger rates, pickups, multi-day itineraries or automatic OTA synchronisation?
5. Are you the decision-maker, and would a monthly software budget around the quoted price be workable if the problem is solved?

Require confirmed workflow fit, an owner decision-maker, a meaningful problem and price awareness before taking calendar time. Unknown answers mean “needs qualification,” not “qualified.” Respectfully decline poor fits and record the reason.

## Social media: 30-day launch

Create one Facebook Page and one professional Instagram account for BookingTours. Use the proposed handles/bios in [the content pack](social-launch-pack.md); availability is unverified. Use a separate business calendar and a verified business reply address. LinkedIn is a secondary agency channel: reuse written posts on an existing authentic founder profile if available; otherwise defer it until the core workflow runs.

The starting audience hypothesis is that these local activity businesses can be reached through Instagram/Facebook and existing operator relationships. Validate it through conversations. No claim is made that their owners are reachable at a particular rate.

### Small publishing schedule

Four weeks, three original assets per week:

- Tuesday: one 30–45-second founder or screen-demo video, on Instagram and Facebook.
- Thursday: one useful carousel, on Instagram and Facebook.
- Friday: one written operator/agency post on Facebook; optional LinkedIn reuse outside the free scheduler.

This is **12 original assets and 20 platform placements**: eight videos, eight carousels and four text posts across the accounts. The wording and shot lists are prepared; videos and carousel images still need production. Suggested posting times are Tuesday/Thursday 12:30 and Friday 09:00 Africa/Johannesburg; these are a starting experiment, not audience-derived best times.

Metricool's [current free plan](https://metricool.com/pricing/) lists one brand and twenty monthly scheduled posts, with LinkedIn excluded. Its [MCP guidance](https://help.metricool.com/mcp-limits-and-plan-requirements-h72jg) says the AI connection works on Free. The plugin was discovered and suggested, **not installed or connected**. Confirm each queue item's quota usage in the actual account, preserve room for an extra calendar week and avoid duplicate uploads. Do not add a paid scheduler just for launch.

### Distribution from zero followers

Posting alone is insufficient. Each week AI prepares:

- Ten thoroughly researched operator accounts, with public evidence and an appropriate way to reach them.
- Two personalised partner proposals for agencies or operator organisations.
- Up to two useful community contributions adapted to the actual group's rules; request administrator permission where required.
- Follow-up drafts for people who explicitly requested help.

The founder or a connected, authorised execution channel sends only the applicable items. No automated follow/unfollow, purchased audiences, mass tagging, fake operator profiles, fabricated testimonials or unsolicited DM sequences. Researching a public profile does not establish marketing consent. The original rollout links the Information Regulator's guidance for lawful approaches.

Social posts speak to business owners, not holidaymakers: open with the activity-owner problem, show the workflow, finish with the checklist or live demo. Avoid using beautiful travel footage without a business reason; it can attract an audience that will never buy the software.

### How we decide whether social works

Record post ID and source when a prospect requests help. Add `utm_source`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=operator_admin_check`, and `utm_content=<post-id>` to verified landing links. UTM parameters do not themselves create analytics or persist through a calendar; also ask “Where did you find us?” and record the answer. Keep one primary source plus assisting posts to prevent double counting.

Review qualified owner conversations, held calls, paid activations and retained collected revenue. Reach, saves and watch time diagnose content; they are not customer-acquisition success.

After four weeks: if we have no qualified owner conversations from the twenty placements and documented distribution attempts, change the audience/offer/distribution before adding volume or paid boosts. If conversations occur but calls do not, improve the invitation and qualification. If calls occur but no one buys, inspect value, fit, price and trust. Small samples guide the next experiment rather than prove a universal conclusion.

## Current rollout and the route to 1,000

| Stage | AI-led work | Founder work and advance gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–7 | Prepare the first three assets, verify five research seeds, reconcile one quote and draft qualification replies. | Own/connect accounts; record once; confirm commercial facts and live payment readiness. Test the calendar before promotion. |
| Days 8–30 | Publish the approved batch once connected, distribute through permitted routes, qualify inbound interest, prepare call briefs and track objections. | Up to four qualified calls/week; oversee first setups. Aim for 2–4 activated operators, potentially still in their free month. |
| Days 31–60 | Repeat content answering actual objections, reconcile invoices/use, prepare two agency proposals and consented case-study questions. | Check whether owners get value and will pay. Do not scale a workflow needing daily rescue. |
| Days 61–90 | Report source-to-payment cohorts, founder minutes and costs; double down on the channel producing retained customers. | Experiment target: 8–12 activated and 6–10 collected-paying businesses. Check initial renewals when eligible. |
| 10 → 50 | Turn successful setup and objection handling into reusable material; track operator referrals and partner-sourced sales. | Fund repeatable setup/support from collected contribution when possible. Keep the cash cap unless explicitly revised. |
| 50 → 100 | Validate a reachable market and at least one productive agency, not merely partner signups. | Review 90-day retention, fully loaded acquisition cost and support capacity. |
| 100 → 1,000 | Expand proven partner, referral and inbound channels; delegate standard delivery. | Requires sufficient market, revenue-funded operations and far more acquisition capacity than four founder calls/week. |

The original rollout includes a conditional growth model crossing 1,000 in month 38. That is **not a promised timetable**: it assumes eventually acquiring 75 paying businesses/month and 2% monthly churn. At 1,000 businesses that churn loses twenty per month. A permanent four-acquisition-per-month process cannot achieve this target with that churn. Social content creates evidence and conversations; productive partners, referrals, retention and funded delivery provide the scale.
