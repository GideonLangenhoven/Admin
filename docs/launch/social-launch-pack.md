## Recording brief: twenty minutes each week

Record vertically in a quiet place, with face and hands visible and space for captions. Plain phone video is sufficient. Avoid filming real customer screens or personal information. No music is required.

For that week's reel: take two natural versions, then answer its three supplementary prompts. AI can use the unused answers for replies and later clips. Stop at twenty minutes; do not polish every sentence. Use your actual experience and flag any number that needs checking.

Production default: 30–45-second vertical video, accurate subtitles, readable opening text and one CTA. Carousels use four slides, one idea per slide, with the full useful text also in the caption. The image dimensions should be checked against the actual selected publishing tool before export.

## The complete lead resource: five-minute booking-admin check

This text can be sent directly in response to CHECK. It does not require a new landing page, gated download or mailing-list subscription.

> **Your five-minute booking-admin check**
>
> Pick one activity and one recent customer enquiry. Keep customer details private.
>
> 1. Could the customer see the correct departure and availability?
> 2. Did they understand the total amount and how to pay?
> 3. Did the payment and booking agree, and could your team see that?
> 4. Did the customer receive the correct meeting point, time and preparation details?
> 5. If plans changed, was the next step clear and recorded?
>
> For each answer write: works / needs checking / needs fixing. Count the manual hand-offs. Choose the most repeated problem for your first improvement.
>
> To estimate its value, use your actual weekly admin time or booking contribution after delivery costs. Avoid counting the same benefit twice. A checklist cannot prove how many sales you've lost.
>
> If you want a second look, reply with your activity, your current process and the step that causes trouble. Please leave out customer names, payment details and private messages. We can tell you whether a BookingTours demonstration looks useful. No obligation to book a call.
