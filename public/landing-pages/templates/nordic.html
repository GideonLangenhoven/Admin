Seasonal Guidance

Weather, honestly

We won't promise sunshine. Nordic coastal weather turns in minutes — here's how each part of the season tends to feel, so you can pack for the range rather than the forecast.

Early Season
Spring

Long light returns and the trails are quiet. Mornings stay cold and mist rolls off the water fast. A waterproof shell and warm layers are non-negotiable.

Peak Season
Summer

The mildest, longest days of the year and the busiest trails. Bright spells and sudden squalls can share an afternoon, so sun and rain gear both earn their place.

Late Season
Autumn

Low light, still water and evening fog over the fjord. Cooler and calmer, with the first real bite in the air. Layer up and start earlier.
