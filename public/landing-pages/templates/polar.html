Isolation Defined

Vastness. Silence. Steel.

To sail polar waters is to yield to the environment. The wind and pack ice are our charts. Inside our reinforced hulls, we navigate the edge of the world. In these extreme latitudes, color disappears, leaving only the gradient of ice, sea, and cloud.

We carry double-rigged Zodiacs and drysuits. No schedules are fixed. We watch for blue ice and humpback flukes.
