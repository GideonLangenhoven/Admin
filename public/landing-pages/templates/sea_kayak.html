The Water

The tide doesn't wait, so neither do we

Most mornings we're on the water before the town wakes — flat calm, low light, the kind of quiet you don't get to choose back on land. The coast changes by the hour here, and knowing which hour to be on it is most of the job.

You don't need experience. You need fifteen minutes of instruction, a boat that fits, and a guide who's read this stretch of coast for years. We handle the rest.

A first-light outing, hour by hour
05:40

Kit fitted on the slipway in the half-dark. The water is glass and nobody is talking much yet.

06:10

First light finds the kelp beds and the whole coast turns amber. This is the hour we build the trip around.

07:00

Back on the sand — coffee going around, salt drying on your hands, the whole day still ahead of you.
