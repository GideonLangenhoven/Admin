Before you paddle out
What should I bring?

Bring sun protection, drinking water, and clothes you don't mind getting splashed. Check your chosen experience's booking details for equipment, clothing, and anything else you need.

Which adventure is right for me?

Morning Kayak offers a fresh start on the water. Sunset Paddle makes the most of the evening light. Read the full activity details, including any age or fitness requirements, before booking.

Where do we meet?

You'll find the meeting point for your chosen departure during booking and in your confirmation. Please check the location and arrival instructions before setting off.

What if the weather changes?

Ocean conditions can affect departures. Check your booking confirmation for the weather and cancellation policy that applies to your experience. You can access an existing reservation through My bookings.
