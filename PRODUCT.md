# Product

## Register

product

## Users

BookingTours primarily serves Southern African adventure and tourism operators. Main admins configure and run the business, while operations staff manage daily bookings, departures, customers, and disruptions. These users are often working quickly, on shared office devices or phones, and may not be highly technical.

## Product Purpose

BookingTours replaces fragmented WhatsApp, spreadsheet, website, and booking-tool workflows with one direct-first operating system. It helps operators sell under their own brand, keep their customer relationships, manage availability and payments, and handle operational problems such as weather cancellations without commission-heavy intermediaries.

## Brand Personality

Warm, natural, and precise. The product should feel like a trusted field guide or well-made outdoor equipment: capable, grounded, and clear rather than corporate or flashy.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, decorative complexity, vague marketing language, purple/violet/indigo accents, emoji used as interface icons, and oversized arbitrary rounding. Operational screens must not prioritize visual novelty over speed or clarity.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next operational action obvious.
2. Use specific language and real system state rather than vague reassurance.
3. Keep frequent workflows fast, forgiving, and predictable across devices.
4. Give color and emphasis a clear job instead of using them decoratively.
5. Preserve operator ownership and tenant identity throughout the product.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. All core workflows must work with a keyboard, maintain visible focus, use sufficient contrast, and avoid relying on color alone. Respect reduced-motion preferences and use native semantics wherever they remain reliable and understandable.
